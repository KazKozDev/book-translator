## Video direction

- **palette system:** paper canvas `bg-primary` #F7F8F9; ink `text-primary` #111827; muted `text-secondary` #6B7280; sole voltage `accent` #C62828; cool UI rule `line` #5B7C99. Hairline geometry only — no shadows, no glow, no purple gradients.
- **motion grammar + reveal model:** long-tail `power3` eases; every piece reveals on its spoken cue; never front-load. Holds prefer stillness over idle drift. Subtle 1px jitter max during holds.
- **rhythm / held frames:** Frame 3 (product intro) and Frame 8 (CTA) are deliberate held reads after their reveals. Frames 4–6 carry the UI evidence energy.
- **negative list:** no browser chrome / scrollbars / fake cursors as decoration; no SaaS purple glow; no cream-terracotta tropes; no invented metrics or user counts; no slideshow dump-then-freeze; no screensaver float.
