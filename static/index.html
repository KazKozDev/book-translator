Format and modes

Upload the source text with → 1 UPLOAD below, then click → PREPARE. The app scans the text, collects names and repeated terms, and fills the list on the left. This step is optional, but it improves translation quality and keeps names consistent across the whole book.

Review the list before you start: delete what you don't need, correct a wrong translation, add what the scan missed. Entries can be edited until you click → 2 START.

Format: source term => translation [| mode]
One entry per line. The => is required. If you omit the mode, inflectable is used.

MODES

exactUse this translation letter for letter, everywhere.
inflectableUse this translation, but let it change form to fit the sentence.
preferredUse this translation where it fits, otherwise the app translates freely.
